Pricing, plainly

Base grooming is priced by size and coat. Everything beyond that is listed below so there are no surprises at pickup.

Some dogs need more time

Anxious dogs, rescues, seniors, and dogs with behavioural concerns are welcome here — they simply take longer and are priced accordingly. Tell us when you call and we will plan the appointment around it.
