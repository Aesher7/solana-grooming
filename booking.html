01 — Why we do it this way

A two-minute call saves an hour

An online form would book the wrong slot for half the dogs that walk in here. A short conversation gets it right the first time.

01

Personalised Care

We ask about coat type, mats, allergies, and how your dog handles being handled — then plan the appointment around the answers.

02

Flexible Scheduling

Nervous dogs get quieter slots. Big double coats get longer ones. A form cannot make that call; we can.

03

Straight Answers

Not sure whether your dog needs a full groom or just a bath? Whether a de-shed is worth it? Ask on the phone and we will tell you honestly, including when the answer is "you don't need that".
